# VolleyTakt Live 0.4.0 Preview17 – Funktionsgegencheck

## Scouting chain
- Previous WOHIN is stored as rally ball position.
- On the next same-side action it preselects WO but does not advance automatically.
- The scout must explicitly confirm or replace the preselected WO.
- Rally end clears the ball position.

## Field UI
- Target courts do not receive base-half highlighting from the selected WHO position.
- action_zone and target_zone use separate visual states.

## Libero
- Libero replacement remains a dedicated player-change workflow.
- P1/P6/P5 are the allowed entry positions in the UI workflow.
- Two-libero selection and direct libero-for-libero replacement remain available.

## Version
- Active UI/runtime/manifests/cache identifiers: 0.4.0 Preview17.
